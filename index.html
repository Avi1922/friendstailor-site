Master Tailoring Since 1994

Founded by Mr. Umesh Gupta, Friends Clothes And Tailor has evolved into a premier custom tailoring destination in Mumbai. Over the last three decades, our enterprise has been built on personal relationships, fit precision, and artisanal craftsmanship.

Whether you require a sharp 3-piece suit for executive presentations, refined groom's attire, or urgent 48-hour delivery for an upcoming event, our master tailors deliver an exact fit tailored to your body profile.
