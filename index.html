OUR STORY · EST. 1994

A small beginning.
A lasting legacy.

Founded in 1994 by Mr. Umesh Gupta in Samta Nagar, Kandivali, Friends Clothes and Tailor began from a very small 5 × 5 feet shop. Over the years, the business steadily grew through quality craftsmanship, customer trust, personalized service and long-term relationships.

1994Where it all began
32+Years of craftsmanship

THE JOURNEY SO FAR

  1. 1994

    The Beginning

    Started from a 5 × 5 feet shop in Samta Nagar.

  2. 2000–2005

    Transition & Development

    A period of establishing and developing the business within the surrounding Samta Nagar area.

  3. 2005–2008

    A New Location

    Moved to a shop approximately 500 metres from the original location.

  4. 2008–2015

    Continued Growth

    Continued serving customers and growing within the Samta Nagar area.

  5. 2016–Present

    Thakur Village

    Established the current business presence in Thakur Village, Mumbai.

From a 5 × 5 feet beginning to 32+ years of customer trust.
